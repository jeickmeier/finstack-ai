Framework-only measurements

- Reducer cost, execute_model_only_completion:
  mean 160.795 microseconds; 95% CI 160.793-160.798 microseconds.
- Model stream throughput, assemble 256 text items:
  mean 14.507 microseconds per stream; approximately 17.65 million items/second;
  95% time CI 14.354-14.661 microseconds.
- Tool stream throughput, assemble 256 cumulative-usage items:
  mean 18.040 microseconds per stream; approximately 14.19 million items/second;
  95% time CI 17.926-18.155 microseconds.
- Idle-session memory, 128 bounded RunTaskOwner instances over one shared
  MemoryJournalStore:
  baseline RSS 1,776 KiB; held RSS 9,504 KiB; incremental RSS 7,728 KiB;
  61,824 bytes per idle session. All owners then joined gracefully with zero
  aborted tasks.

Interpretation

These numbers measure only finstack framework work. They intentionally exclude
provider, network, durable-store, and workflow-engine latency. Criterion smoke
intervals are suitable for establishing that the hot paths execute and for
host-local comparison; they are not portable cross-host regression thresholds.
The idle-memory value is an incremental process RSS observation, not an
allocator-exact ownership attribution.
